Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Multimedia Solution Cafe ("MSC", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website, contact us or enquire about our services.

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

We may use the information you provide to:

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate our website or deliver a requested service, or when required by applicable law.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by us. However, no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose or as required by law.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention requirements.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their independent privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
