GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

A few quick answers about MSC digital wedding albums and online invitations. Contact our team if you have a specific requirement.

What is a digital wedding E-Album?

A digital wedding E-Album is an online photo gallery that lets a couple share selected wedding photographs through a link, so family and friends can view the memories on their devices.

What is a Web Wedding Invitation?

It is a personalised invitation webpage that can display the couple's names, event schedule, date, venue and other approved details. Share the page link with your guests.

Can guests open these links on mobile phones?

Yes. The digital experience is designed to be mobile-friendly and can be opened in a modern mobile browser.

Does MSC serve customers outside Bhagalpur?

MSC welcomes digital wedding service enquiries across Bihar and Jharkhand, including Bhagalpur, Kahalgaon, Patna, Munger, Jamalpur and nearby areas. Confirm project availability directly with the team.

How do I get pricing and delivery details?

Contact MSC with your event date and requirements. Pricing, features, hosting duration and delivery timeline are discussed and confirmed for each project.
