MSC Tech Store FAQs

Can I request a custom gaming PC build?

Yes. Share your use case, budget and component preferences through the custom PC enquiry form. MSC can discuss a suitable configuration, subject to component availability and final confirmation.

Can I ask about PC components and accessories?

Yes. Contact MSC to enquire about processors, motherboards, RAM, storage, graphics cards, monitors and computer accessories. Availability and prices must be confirmed before purchase.

How do I enquire about cameras, CCTV or computer services?

Use the WhatsApp enquiry links on this page to describe the product or service you need. Installation, service scope, pricing and delivery arrangements will be confirmed directly.

Does submitting the custom PC form place a confirmed order?

No. The form opens a WhatsApp enquiry. Your order is not confirmed until the configuration, component availability, final price and delivery details have been agreed.
